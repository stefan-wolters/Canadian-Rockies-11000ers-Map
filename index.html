Map of the 11000ers of the Canadian Rockies


Made by Stefan Wolters


The 11000ers are a group of mountains in the Canadian Rockies, categorized by their height above 11 000 feet. While there is ambiguity as what counts as a peak above 11 000 feet, there are roughly 50-58 peaks above 11 000, depending on your data.

The list of 11000ers have become elusive goals for climbing and mountaineering, as only ~25 people have climbed all of the 11000ers in the Canadian Rockies.

On this page is a map of 50 of the major 11000ers, each with data and images.
